Need to Know

Your comprehensive optical guide to frames, lenses, and precision vision

UV400 vs. Polarized: All Shivam Optics sunglasses offer certified 100% UV400 protection against both UVA and UVB rays. Our polarized editions incorporate an acoustic multi-layer iodine filter to eliminate blinding glare from water, tarmac, and snow without distorting natural colors.

Face Shape Fit: Round faces benefit from angular Geometric and Rectangle frames; square jawlines harmonize with soft Round and Aviator silhouettes.

Lens Options: You can select Zero Power (Fashion / Blue Light), Single Vision (Distance or Reading), or Freeform Digital Progressives (no line bifocals with wide corridor transition).

Thin High Index: For prescriptions over ±2.50, our 1.61, 1.67, and 1.74 ultra-thin high-index lenses prevent lens thickness and heavy weight.

We provide certified Silicone Hydrogel breathable daily, bi-weekly, and monthly disposable contact lenses from Alcon, Bausch & Lomb, and Acuvue.

Ensure you have a recent corneal curvature (BC) and diameter measurement. Our optometrist team verifies each prescription before fulfillment.

Shield Against Digital Eye Strain: Shivam Optics Blue-Block lenses filter out 98% of harmful 410-455nm high-energy blue radiation emitted by laptops, tablets, and smartphone displays.

Available in both zero-power (plano) and prescription formats, fitted with anti-smudge hydrophobic oleophobic coatings.

How do I submit my prescription? You can type it during checkout, upload a photo of your doctor's slip, or send it directly to our optometrist on WhatsApp (097799 90207) after placing your order.

What is the warranty policy? 1 Year Comprehensive Warranty on all frames and lens anti-reflective coatings against manufacturing defects.

Returns & Exchanges: 14-day hassle-free doorstep return and exchange guarantee if the fit or power is not 100% satisfactory.
